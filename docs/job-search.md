## 6. Outreach Conversion Health & Metric Tracking

Track your prospecting funnel weekly in a simple spreadsheet:

```text
Total Connection Requests Sent: [ _____ ]
Accepted Connections:          [ _____ ]  -> Acceptance Rate = Accepted / Sent (Target: >= 35%)
Messages Sent:                 [ _____ ]
Replies Received:              [ _____ ]  -> Reply Rate = Replies / Messages (Target: >= 15%)
Conversations Started:         [ _____ ]
Interviews Booked:             [ _____ ]  -> Interview Rate = Interviews / Sent (Target: >= 5%)
Offers Received:               [ _____ ]
```

### 🚩 When to Pivot Your Outreach Strategy:
* **If Connection Acceptance Rate is $<20\%$**: Your LinkedIn headline or photo needs refinement. Shorten your connection note to $<200$ chars.
* **If Reply Rate is $<10\%$**: Your post-connection message is too long or generic. Make sure the 2nd sentence specifically references the company's product and links the matching project from your portfolio.
* **If you get replies but no interviews**: Focus on sending direct code links (`github.com/SoufyanRachdi/...`) and proposing a low-pressure 10-minute introductory call.
