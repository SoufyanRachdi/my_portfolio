## 1. Outreach Strategy & Message Templates

### Strategy Overview
1. **Step 1: Short Connection Note ($\le 300$ characters)**: Never paste a long cover letter into a connection request. Be polite, mention a shared tech interest or their company product, and state that you are a recent ISIMG Computer Science graduate.
2. **Step 2: Value-Driven Message (After Connection Accepted)**: Send a concise 3-paragraph message highlighting:
   - Your background (**Computer Science Graduate @ ISIMG**)
   - One **hyper-relevant project** solving problems matching their product
   - Direct link to your **Portfolio & GitHub**
   - A low-friction question about junior engineering openings.
